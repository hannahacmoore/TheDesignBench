Single Gang Light Switch Cover

A clean, minimal single-gang switch cover plate, available with a decora/rocker-style cutout, a toggle-style cutout, or as a blank cover (no cutout) for unused or decorative switches. Each version includes a standard size and a slightly oversized "Large" version (+0.5cm on all sides) for covering paint buildup, chipped edges, or slightly oversized wall openings. Pick your cutout in the View Design menu, or use the links on each style card below.
